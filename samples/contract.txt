SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Term and Termination
This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months.
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days prior written notice.
The Customer may terminate immediately in case of a material breach by the Supplier that remains uncured for thirty (30) days.

2. Payment
The Customer shall pay all invoices within fifteen (15) days of receipt.
Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.
All fees are non-refundable.

3. Limitation of Liability
The Supplier's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid in the preceding three (3) months.
The Supplier shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
The Customer bears full responsibility for any data loss resulting from its own use of the deliverables.

4. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret for a period of five (5) years after termination.
The Supplier may disclose confidential information to subcontractors without prior written consent of the Customer.

5. Intellectual Property
All intellectual property rights in the deliverables shall vest in the Supplier.
The Customer is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the deliverables solely for internal business purposes.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of Serbia.
Any disputes shall be resolved by the courts of Belgrade.
